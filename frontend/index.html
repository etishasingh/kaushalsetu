      ABOUT KAUSHALSETU

      Career guidance built around evidence.

      KaushalSetu is designed for a learner and their family to explore vocational options together — not to make an automatic life decision for them.

      01Understand

      Your interests, education, experience and practical constraints shape the recommendation set.

      02Compare

      Five ranked paths are presented with eligibility, training and labour-market evidence.

      03Investigate

      Every career detail page highlights missing information and questions worth asking before enrolment.

      The system does not guarantee a job, salary or admission. Current local availability and fees should always be verified.

      DATA & PROVENANCE

      Where the evidence comes from.

      The project's recommendation and counselling layers are built from the following datasets and official/public sources.

      National Qualifications Register (NQR)Qualifications, NSQF level, eligibility, duration, competencies and official qualification records.nqr.gov.in ↗
      Skill India Digital Hub (SIDH)Training-centre/provider evidence and local availability records used in the project sample.skillindiadigital.gov.in ↗
      National Career Service (NCS)Employment/job evidence and career-centre/referral context included in the datasets.ncs.gov.in ↗
      DGT / Ministry & survey contextPlacement and earnings context is labelled as a benchmark, not a guaranteed individual salary.
      Project datasetsqualifications.csv • training_providers.csv • ncs_jobs.csv • placement_earnings.csv • career_referral_directory.csv • counselling datasets
